## Observable convergence

Relative changes below are fractions, not percentages. Photon totals include
the number and energy of both sources and of each source separately; bins
include both count and energy in [0,1e14,1e15,1e16,infinity) eV. These remain
deterministic diagnostic bins. Their subsequent use as statistical acceptance
bins is defined separately in the [frozen protocol](../../tests/crpropa/ACCEPTANCE.md).

| Comparison | Maximum photon-total change | Maximum photon-bin change |
|---|---:|---:|
| Grid17 to grid33, BDF | 1.43e-10 | 1.27e-11 |
| Grid33 to grid65, BDF | 2.04e-10 | 1.84e-11 |
| Grid17 to grid33, tighter Radau | 2.06e-13 | 2.58e-13 |
| Grid33 to grid65, tighter Radau | 6.40e-14 | 8.80e-14 |
| Independent quadrature32 to 64 | 3.45e-11 | 1.96e-11 |
| Native background step .0025 to .00125 | 4.13e-5 | 2.05e-5 |
| BDF versus tighter Radau, all cases | 8.49e-10 | 7.70e-11 |

The apparent BDF grid changes are smaller than the BDF/Radau path discrepancy
and are strongly reduced when comparing Radau solutions across grids. This
indicates a path-solver accuracy floor in these BDF comparisons, rather than
an observable energy-grid instability. It would be incorrect to infer a
strict asymptotic order from the BDF grid differences alone.

Lepton count/energy controls show a maximum BDF/Radau change of 3.15e-9 and
a maximum native-background refinement change of 5.95e-5. Extremely rare
isotope means require separate care: a value merely above one coordinate's
atol is not guaranteed relatively accurate after summing many coordinates.
For example, the largest archived relative grid33/65 isotope-count change,
6.52e-7, belongs to P31 with mean yield about 4.30e-16 per primary. It does
not describe an experimentally resolvable isotope yield at the history cap.

All numbers in the convergence table are observed differences. They are not
rigorous global error bounds, statistical confidence intervals, or newly
chosen allocations of the agreed acceptance budgets.

## Conservation and energy-state populations

Across all 44 BDF/Radau solutions the maximum absolute errors are 5.69e-14
in expected baryon number and 2.49e-14 in charge including beta leptons.
The largest relative error in nuclear-plus-lepton mean energy is 1.23e-15.
As required by the compatibility model, the additional non-debited photons
are excluded from this energy invariant. The most negative scaled component
is -2.52e-30; such roundoff values were retained, not clipped into rare yields.

The integral/bin conclusions do not establish fine nuclear energy shapes.
An additional diagnostic restricts the fine-grid nodal populations onto the
coarse grid using positive count/mean-energy-preserving linear weights.
For Cr52, the L1 population difference conditional on this isotope's yield
is about 0.45-0.47% for grid17/33 and 1.25-1.31% for grid33/65. The sequence
is not monotonic. The largest absolute contribution is 1.35e-4 Cr52 nuclei
per injected primary; the summed nodal L1 change over isotopes reaches
1.68e-4 per primary for grid33/65.

These are differences of restricted nodal representations, not errors in
fixed physical spectral bins. Remapping of narrow, nearly discrete nuclear
energy features can change these representations while leaving integrated
yield, mean energy and smooth photon rewards stable. Consequently this audit
does not certify nuclear peak shapes or apply the 5% spectral budget to the
nodal L1 metric. Fixed physical nuclear bins would need their own refinement
check if included in the final protocol.

## Statistical limits and next acceptance gate

First moments do not supply complete-history variances or simultaneous
confidence intervals. At the cap of 200000 primaries, the independent means
imply only about 290/450 decay photons for CMB/CMB+Inoue12, and about
0.43/0.90 beta-minus electrons. These are expected counts, not an uncertainty
model. A deterministic mean precise to many digits cannot make such sparse
Monte Carlo outputs precise; unresolved outputs must remain inconclusive.

The full numerical matrix clears the computational/convergence check for
the reported integral observables and diagnostic photon bins at this point.
The subsequent [frozen initial-point protocol](../../tests/crpropa/ACCEPTANCE.md)
now defines observable families, bins, normalizations, numerical envelopes,
fresh seeds and ensemble sizes. Its final Monte Carlo campaign remains to
be executed. Fine nuclear spectra and other injection/path points remain
outside this conclusion. Simultaneous 99% confidence, 1% integral / 5%
spectral tolerances and the 200000-primary cap are unchanged.
